Who we are

Communities we're proud to run.

We own and operate all three of our communities ourselves — no distant corporate office, no revolving-door management companies.

Our approach

We put more back in. Most operators our size collect rent and defer the maintenance. We do the opposite — better roads, better grounds, and local managers who answer the phone. When something needs fixing, it gets fixed.

Straight pricing, no games. Every home has a fair, upfront price. No pressure tactics, no bait-and-switch, no surprises at signing. What you see on this site is what it costs.

Ownership that means something. Every home in our communities is for sale — never for rent. You own the home outright; you lease the homesite it sits on. That means your monthly cost stays low, and the home is yours to improve, keep, or sell.

Local and reachable. Our communities are managed by people who are actually here — showings, questions, and maintenance are handled by a team you'll know by name.
