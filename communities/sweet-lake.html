About this community

Sweet Lake sits in a serene wooded setting with beautiful views of the lake, on E US 12 just outside Sturgis. It's the kind of place where you can actually relax — quiet streets, well-kept grounds, and a friendly, settled neighborhood.

You get peaceful, country-style living with the convenience of Sturgis a short drive away: grocery stores, restaurants, schools, and everything else you need day to day.

The community is professionally managed by a local team that answers the phone, keeps the grounds maintained, and treats residents fairly.
